# Money 101 trailer

## The idea

"Quiero que me propongas unos temas a tratar y que crees un vídeo de presentación breve para ver
el formato": a short video that presents the channel's first series, Money 101 (teaching, on
personal finance), made to try the new format: the new presenter, a look for young adults and
the series' colour.

## Verdict

**make it**, as a trailer (`kind: trailer`): under a minute, no figures, so no sources. It is
the format's test piece first and the series' trailer second; whether it is published is the
user's call once the format is settled.

## The angle

School skipped how money works, people decide about it every day anyway, and this series
explains it one idea at a time, with sources and without telling anyone what to buy.

## Who it is for, and what they will know at the end

People in their twenties who are starting to earn, borrow and save, and were never taught how
any of it works. At the end they know what the series is, what it covers and what it will not
do (hype, advice).

## The facts

None: the trailer states no figure, date or quotation. "Odds are, nobody taught you this in
school" is said as a likelihood, not as a statistic.

## The strongest objection

That "we explain, you decide" is a promise the episodes must keep: a series that teaches
personal finance slides into advice easily. Each episode's brief has to hold the line (how a
thing works and what changes with it, never what to do).

## Outline

1. 01-hook: the presenter, large. School skipped this: how interest works, where a paycheck
   goes, why prices keep rising.
2. 02-guessing: three everyday money questions as cards; most are answered by guessing.
3. 03-series: the series' name, large, and what it is in two lines. The presenter points at it.
4. 04-topics: six subjects as tiles, each with a small drawing, coming in as they are named.
5. 05-rules: three rules, counted on the presenter's fingers: sources, no hype, no advice.
6. 06-close: start from zero; subscribe.

## The Short

All six scenes, in order: the trailer is short enough to be its own Short.

## Open questions for the user

- The series' name (Money 101 is a working name) and its colour.
- Whether the presenter has a name and says it.
- The six subjects named in 04-topics are the ones proposed for the first episodes: if the list
  changes, that scene changes with it.

The Market Hub is officially live.

Every morning I used to open five websites to understand what had happened in the market and how my portfolio was doing. So I built one place that brings it all together.

Inside My Hub, your private area:
— Your portfolio with your average cost, what you're up or down, and how it's split
— AI that reads a company's numbers and its results release, and builds the charts you ask for
— Five tools: Fundamentals Lab, Earnings Radar, Playground, Peer Map and Options Lab
— A monthly portfolio competition. The first one is November, and entries are open until October 31

It's free: themarkethub.app (link in bio). The full launch film is on our YouTube channel.

It explains; it never tells you what to buy or sell. The portfolio in the video is a demo account.

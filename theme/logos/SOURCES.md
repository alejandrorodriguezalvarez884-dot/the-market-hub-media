# Company logos

Used on the slides when a company is named (the owner's decision, 2026-11-02), always from this
folder, never loaded from the network. Each is one file, `<name>.svg`, coloured with the brand's
own colour.

- From Simple Icons 13.21.0 (https://simpleicons.org, CC0-1.0), with the colour Simple Icons
  gives each brand: google, googlecloud, meta, amazon, intel, apple, nvidia, amd, samsung, tesla,
  netflix, oracle, palantir, broadcom, sandisk, walmart, cocacola, and qualcomm, airbnb and arm
  (added 2026-11-05 for that day's open reel); sandisk, walmart, cocacola and arm are wordmarks:
  their viewBox is cropped to the drawing, so they fill the tile.
- Drawn here (Simple Icons does not carry it): microsoft, the four coloured squares; and, as plain
  wordmarks in the brand's colours (the name set in type, not the companies' own artwork): micron,
  skhynix, costco and pg (P&G on a blue disc); and coreweave, iren, cipher (Cipher Digital, formerly
  Cipher Mining) and nebius, added 2026-11-04 for the midterms reel, in colours close to the brands'.

The logos are the companies' trademarks: they name the company a slide talks about, nothing more.
A missing one is added the same way; a company with none here is written as text.

# Context — "do the numbers add up?"

1. **CoreWeave depreciates its GPU servers over 6 years.** 10-K for 2025 (filed 2026-03-02), Note "Overview and Summary of Significant Accounting Policies": technology equipment useful life "6 years" (straight-line); effective Jan 1, 2023 it changed the useful life of data-center computing equipment "from five to six years" (2023 effect: $20M lower expenses).
   - Policies: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1769628/000176962826000104/R28.htm
   - Details table: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1769628/000176962826000104/R43.htm
   - Main 10-K: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1769628/000176962826000104/crwv-20251231.htm
   - The Q2 2026 10-Q says no material changes to accounting policies since the 10-K. (The Q3 2026 10-Q was not yet on EDGAR when checked.)

2. **CoreWeave's debt and interest.** Balance sheet at Sept 30, 2026: total debt ≈ $46.9B (recourse 9,183 + 32,417; non-recourse 1,389 + 3,884; $M, my sum; excludes finance leases), up from ≈ $35.1B at June 30, 2026 (10-Q). Interest expense, net Q3 2026: $846M (Q3 2025: ~$310M), versus GAAP operating income of $207M. Q4 2026 interest guided to $1.05B–$1.15B.
   - https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1769628/000176962826000461/coreweave3q26earningspress.htm
   - https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1769628/000176962826000366/crwv-20260630.htm

3. **D&A vs revenue at CoreWeave.** Q3 2026 depreciation & amortization $1,889M on revenue of $3,661M (≈52%); Q3 capex $9,441M; FY2026 capex guidance $37B–$39B vs FY2026 revenue guidance $13.0B–$13.3B. (Same press release.)

4. **Big-four hyperscaler capex 2026.** Fortune, Oct 31, 2026, after the late-October earnings: Amazon $220B, Alphabet up to $205B, Microsoft ~ $170B, Meta up to $145B — together up to ~$740B (my sum of the upper figures); with SpaceX (incl. xAI, up to $88B) the group is "as much as $828 billion". Banks (Evercore, BofA) see AI capex around $1 trillion in 2027.
   - https://fortune.com/2026/10/31/big-tech-ai-capital-spending-spree/
   - Note: Alphabet's guide is a range $195B–$205B and Meta's $135B–$145B (per search summaries of CNBC Oct 29, 2026 coverage — not read directly), so the four-company total is ~$720B–$740B depending on range ends. Definitions (finance leases or not) differ by company.

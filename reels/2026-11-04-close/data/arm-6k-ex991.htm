Arm Holdings plc Reports Results for the Second Quarter of the Fiscal Year Ending 2027

Cambridge, England, Nov. 4, 2026: Arm Holdings plc (NASDAQ: ARM), the company that is building the future of computing, has today published a letter to its shareholders containing the company’s results for its second quarter of fiscal year 2027, which ended Sept. 30, 2026. The letter is available on its investor relations website (https://investors.arm.com/financials/quarterly-annual-results). The shareholder letter will also be furnished to the Securities and Exchange Commission (SEC) on a Form 6-K and will be available on the SEC website at http://www.sec.gov.

Arm will host an audio webcast to discuss its results at 14:00 PT / 17:00 ET / 22:00 GMT today, Nov. 4. The live webcast will be available at https://edge.media-server.com/mmc/p/zyzi4u5w/ and a replay will be at https://investors.arm.com/financials/quarterly-annual-results.

About Arm
Arm is the foundational platform for AI, delivering power-efficient, high-performance computing that touches 100 percent of the connected global population. Arm has the world’s largest computing ecosystem, including over 30 million developers, and together we are building the future of AI on Arm.

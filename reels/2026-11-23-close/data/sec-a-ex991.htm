Agilent Reports Fourth-Quarter Fiscal Year 2026 Financial Results
Delivers strong Q4 on broad strength; initiates FY27 and first quarter FY27 guidance on continued momentum

Fourth-quarter fiscal year 2026

•Revenue of $2.04 billion for the fourth quarter ended Oct. 31, 2026, representing growth of 9.6% reported and up 8.3% core(1) compared with the fourth quarter of fiscal year 2025.

•GAAP operating margin of 25.0%, expanded by 120 basis points year-over-year and 140 basis points sequentially.

•Non-GAAP operating margin(2) of 30.0% (including an approximately 120 basis point net benefit from tariff refunds), expanded by 280 basis points year-over-year and 170 basis points sequentially.

•GAAP net income of $451 million; earnings per share (EPS) of $1.59, an increase of 4% from the fourth quarter of fiscal year 2025.

•Non-GAAP net income(3) of $526 million (including a $20 million net benefit from tariff refunds); non-GAAP EPS(3) of $1.86 (including a $0.07 net benefit from tariff refunds), an increase of 17% from the fourth quarter of fiscal year 2025.

Fiscal year 2026

•Revenue of $7.55 billion for the fiscal year ended Oct. 31, 2026, representing growth of 8.7% reported and up 6.6% core(1) compared with fiscal year 2025.

•GAAP operating margin of 22.6%, expanded by 130 basis points year-over-year.

•Non-GAAP operating margin(2) of 27.4% (including an approximately 60 basis point net benefit from tariff refunds), expanded by 170 basis points year-over-year.

•GAAP net income of $1,457 million; earnings per share (EPS) of $5.15, an increase of 13% from fiscal year 2025.

•Non-GAAP net income(3) of $1,794 million (including a $37 million net benefit from tariff refunds); non-GAAP EPS(3) of $6.34 (including a $0.13 net benefit from tariff refunds), an increase of 13% from fiscal year 2025.

Fiscal year 2027 and first-quarter outlook
•Fiscal year 2027 revenue is expected in the range of $8.00 billion to $8.15 billion, representing a range of up 6.0% to 8.0% reported and up 5.0% to 7.0% core(1)(5).

•Excluding the net benefit of tariff refunds recognized in fiscal year 2026, non-GAAP fiscal year 2027 operating margin expansion(2) at the midpoint of core revenue guidance is expected to be approximately 70 basis points higher relative to 26.8% in fiscal year 2026. Including the net benefit of tariff refunds recognized in fiscal year 2026, non-GAAP fiscal year 2027 operating margin expansion(2) at the midpoint of core revenue growth guidance is expected to be approximately 10 basis points higher.

•Non-GAAP fiscal year 2027 EPS(4) is expected in the range of $6.73 to $6.86, an increase of 7.3% at the midpoint compared with non-GAAP fiscal year 2026 EPS of $6.34, which included the net benefit of tariff refunds. Excluding the net benefit of tariff refunds recognized in fiscal year 2026, EPS growth is expected to be 8.5% to 10.5%.

•First-quarter 2027 revenue is expected in the range of $1.96 billion to $2.00 billion, growth of 8.8% to 11.0% reported and up 7.2% to 9.4% core(1)(5). Non-GAAP EPS(4) is expected in the range of $1.54 to $1.57 per share.

•The fiscal year 2027 and first-quarter outlook does not include any future benefit from tariff refunds.


SANTA CLARA, California, November 23, 2026 — Agilent Technologies Inc. (NYSE: A) today reported revenue of $2.04 billion for the fourth quarter ended Oct. 31, 2026, representing growth of 9.6% reported and up 8.3% core(1) compared with the fourth quarter of fiscal year 2025.

Fourth-quarter GAAP net income was $451 million, or $1.59 per share. This compares with $434 million, or $1.53 per share, in the fourth quarter of fiscal year 2025. Non-GAAP net income(3) was $526 million (including a $20 million net benefit from tariff refunds), or $1.86 per share (including
2

a $0.07 net benefit from tariff refunds), during the quarter, compared with $452 million, or $1.59 per share, during the year-ago quarter.

"We closed FY26 well ahead of our initial outlook, with momentum building quarter by quarter and robust growth across all our end markets, driven by our strong execution and the compounding benefits of the Ignite Operating System on both the top and bottom lines," said Agilent CEO Padraig McDonnell. "We enter 2027 from a position of strength, building on a track record of delivering on our commitments. Our strong commercial and operational execution, relentless focus on advancing customer-led innovation, and deep customer intimacy underpin our confidence in outpacing our end markets. As a trusted partner to our customers, Agilent is well equipped to capture existing and emerging opportunities, including those unlocked by AI and the buildout of autonomous labs, positioning our customers for success in the connected lab ecosystem of the future."

Financial Highlights

Life Sciences and Diagnostics Markets Group

The Life Sciences and Diagnostics Markets Group (LDG) reported fourth-quarter revenue of $843 million, growth of 12% reported and 9% core(1) year-over-year. LDG’s operating margin for the quarter was 25.9%.

Agilent CrossLab Group

The Agilent CrossLab Group (ACG) reported fourth-quarter revenue of $799 million, growth of 6% reported and 6% core(1) year-over-year. ACG’s operating margin for the quarter was 34.5%.

Applied Markets Group

The Applied Markets Group (AMG) reported fourth-quarter revenue of $398 million, growth of 13% reported and 13% core(1) year-over-year. AMG’s operating margin for the quarter was 29.4%.

We provide non-GAAP net income and non-GAAP net income per share amounts in order to provide meaningful supplemental information regarding our operational performance and our prospects for the future. These supplemental measures exclude, among other things, charges related to restructuring and other related costs, asset impairments, amortization of intangibles, transformational initiatives, acquisition and integration costs, net (gain) loss on equity securities and pension settlement loss.

Restructuring and other related costs include incremental expenses incurred in the period associated with restructuring programs, usually aimed at changes in business and/or cost structure. Such costs may include one-time termination benefits including acceleration of stock-based compensation expense, facility-related costs and contract termination fees.

Asset impairments include assets that have been written down to their fair value.

Intangible amortization refers to the systematic expensing of the cost of finite‑lived intangible assets over their estimated useful lives. Such assets, which include acquired patents, trademarks, customer lists, and other identifiable intangibles, are amortized to match the pattern in which their economic benefits are consumed. If the useful life is not reliably determinable, amortization is calculated on a straight‑line basis over the best estimate of that life.

Transformational initiatives include expenses associated with targeted cost reduction activities such as manufacturing transfers including costs to move manufacturing, site consolidations, legal entity and other business reorganizations, insourcing or outsourcing of activities. Such costs may include move and relocation costs, one-time termination benefits and other one-time reorganization costs. Included in this category are also expenses associated with company programs to transform our product lifecycle management (PLM) system and human resources and financial systems.

Acquisition and integration costs include all incremental expenses incurred to effect a business combination. Such acquisition costs may include advisory, legal, tax, accounting, valuation, and other professional or consulting fees. Such integration costs may include expenses directly related to integration of business and facility operations, the transfer of assets and intellectual property, information technology systems and infrastructure and other employee-related costs.

Net (gain) loss on equity securities relates to the realized and unrealized mark-to-market adjustments for our marketable and non-marketable equity securities.

Pension settlement loss relates to the relief of the US Retirement Plan pension obligation due to increased lump sum payouts exceeding the threshold for settlement accounting under US GAAP. For the fiscal year ended October 31, 2025, it also includes settlement loss that resulted from the transfer of the Netherlands defined benefit plan to an unaffiliated insurance company.

Other includes certain legal costs and settlements, consulting costs, special compliance costs, acceleration of stock-based compensation expense, donation to Agilent Foundation and other miscellaneous adjustments.

Our management uses non-GAAP measures to evaluate the performance of our core businesses, to estimate future core performance and to compensate employees. Since management finds this measure to be useful, we believe that our investors benefit from seeing our results “through the eyes” of management in addition to seeing our GAAP results. This information facilitates our management’s internal comparisons to our historical operating results as well as to the operating results of our competitors.

Our management recognizes that items such as amortization of intangibles can have a material impact on our cash flows and/or our net income. Our GAAP financial statements including our statement of cash flows portray those effects. Although we believe it is useful for investors to see core performance free of special items, investors should understand that the excluded items are actual expenses that may impact the cash available to us for other uses. To gain a complete picture of all effects on the company’s profit and loss from any and all events, management does (and investors should) rely upon the GAAP income statement. The non-GAAP numbers focus instead upon the core business of the company, which is only a subset, albeit a critical one, of the company’s performance.

Readers are reminded that non-GAAP numbers are merely a supplement to, and not a replacement for, GAAP financial measures. They should be read in conjunction with the GAAP financial measures.

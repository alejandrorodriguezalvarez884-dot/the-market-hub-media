Gap Inc. Reports Third Quarter Fiscal 2026 Results

Net sales up 2% versus last year; comparable sales flat

Exceeded operating margin expectations

Earnings per share up over 30% versus last year

Provides updated outlook for full year

Company separately announces Ashley Kechter as Athleta's next President and CEO


SAN FRANCISCO – November 19, 2026 – Gap Inc. (NYSE: GAP), the largest specialty apparel company in the U.S. with a purpose-driven house of iconic brands including Old Navy, Gap, Banana Republic, and Athleta, today reported financial results for its third quarter ended October 31, 2026.

“In the third quarter, we grew net sales 2%, exceeded our profit expectations and gained market share,” said President and Chief Executive Officer, Richard Dickson. “Gap posted another outstanding quarter, and we delivered meaningful sequential improvement at Old Navy.”

Dickson continued, “While demand softened in October and early November, amidst warmer weather, we are encouraged by the more recent improvement in trends as temperatures have cooled. As we move through the holiday season, we remain focused on delivering compelling product assortments and culturally relevant marketing across our brands, supported by a strong value proposition for our customers. We continue to operate with rigor and discipline, with the strength of our balance sheet giving us the flexibility to continue investing in the capabilities and growth opportunities that we believe will create long-term value, while returning cash to shareholders.”

Third Quarter Fiscal 2026 – Financial Results
•Net sales of $4.0 billion were up 2% compared to last year. Comparable sales were flat.
•Store sales increased 1% compared to last year. The Company ended the quarter with nearly 3,500 store locations in about 40 countries, of which 2,482 were Company-operated.
•Online sales increased 3% compared to last year and represented 40% of total net sales.
•Gross margin of 42.5% increased 10 basis points versus last year on a reported basis including benefits related to the recovery of tariffs imposed under the International


Emergency Economic Powers Act ("IEEPA"). Adjusted gross margin excluding this benefit was 42.4%, flat versus last year.
•Merchandise margin increased 50 basis points versus last year on a reported basis. Adjusted for the IEEPA tariff recovery, merchandise margin increased 40 basis points including a benefit from lower tariff rates, partially offset by higher fuel costs. The underlying merchandise margin expansion reflects another quarter of strength at the Gap brand, partially offset by lower average unit retail resulting from planned pricing adjustments at Old Navy as well as higher promotional activity at Old Navy and Athleta.
•Rent, occupancy, and depreciation ("ROD") as a percent of sales deleveraged 40 basis points versus last year.
•Operating expense was $1.3 billion or 32.6% of net sales.
•Operating income was $396 million and operating margin was 9.9%
•Adjusted operating income was $393 million and adjusted operating margin was 9.8%, excluding the impact of the IEEPA tariff recovery.
•The effective tax rate was 25.6% and adjusted effective tax rate was 25.8%, which excludes the tax impact of the IEEPA tariff recovery and related interest income.
•Net income was $311 million and diluted earnings per share were $0.86.
•Adjusted net income was $296 million and adjusted diluted earnings per share were $0.82, excluding the impact of the IEEPA tariff recovery and related interest income.
•Diluted weighted average shares were 360 million.

Balance Sheet and Cash Flow Highlights
•Ended the quarter with cash, cash equivalents and short-term investments of $3.0 billion, an increase of 18% from the prior year. This includes approximately $536 million related to IEEPA tariff refunds including interest received year-to-date and $263 million related to a legal settlement net of a charitable donation that occurred during the first quarter.
•Year-to-date net cash from operating activities was $1.3 billion. Free cash flow, defined as net cash from operating activities less purchases of property and equipment, was $815 million year-to-date.
•Ending inventory of $2.3 billion was down 6% compared to last year.
•Capital expenditures were $483 million year-to-date.

Shareholder Returns
•Returned $788 million of cash to shareholders in the form of dividends and share repurchases year-to-date.
•Share Repurchases:
◦Year-to-date, the Company has repurchased $601 million shares through a combination of purchases in the open market and an accelerated share repurchase program completed during the second quarter.
◦The Company has $399 million remaining under its existing share repurchase authorization.







•Dividends:
◦Year-to-date, the Company has paid $187 million in dividends, including $62 million paid during the third quarter of fiscal 2026, reflecting a quarterly dividend of $0.175 per share, up 6% from the prior year.
◦The Company’s Board of Directors approved a fourth quarter fiscal 2026 dividend of $0.175 per share.

Tariff Update
Year-to-date, the Company recorded a $420 million adjustment to cost of goods sold and a $21 million adjustment to interest income related to the net IEEPA tariff recovery. The Company's adjusted outlook excludes these impacts.
The Company's current outlook reflects updated tariff rate assumptions to incorporate a roughly 10% incremental tariff rate from February 24, 2026 through the end of this week reflecting the rates currently in place, with an increase to an incremental high-teens rate for the balance of the year. The prior outlook assumed an incremental high-teens rate beginning in September. This update is expected to provide approximately $46 million of incremental net tariff relief to full-year gross profit and operating income, or approximately 30 basis points of benefit to full-year gross margin and operating margin and is reflected in the Company's current outlook.

Fiscal 2026 Outlook
The Company's updated full-year outlook factors in quarter-to-date performance reflecting a slower than expected start to the fourth quarter, followed by recent improvement as weather has normalized.

On a reported basis, the Company now expects full year diluted earnings per share to be approximately $3.71 to $3.76.

About Gap Inc.
Gap Inc., a purpose-driven house of iconic brands, is the largest specialty apparel company in America. Its Old Navy, Gap, Banana Republic, and Athleta brands offer clothing, accessories, and lifestyle products for men, women and children worldwide through Company-operated and franchise stores and e-commerce sites. Through Encore, its cross-brand membership program, Gap Inc. connects members across its portfolio to rewards, benefits and exclusive experiences. Since 1969, Gap Inc. has created products and experiences that shape culture, while doing right by employees, communities and the planet through its commitment to bridge gaps to create a better world.

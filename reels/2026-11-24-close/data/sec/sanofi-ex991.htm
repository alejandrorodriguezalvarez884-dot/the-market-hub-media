Sanofi to acquire Insmed in strategic step that strengthens long-term future

 

  •  

Insmed is a transformative acquisition providing strong strategic fit that complements Sanofi’s immunology and rare disease portfolio

 

  •  

Sanofi will gain access to two first-in-disease commercialized products and pipeline assets, strengthening its mid-to long-term growth outlook and its position in Respiratory

 

  •  

Sanofi will pay $175 per share, for a total consideration of $41.0 billion / €37.6 billion in cash. The transaction is expected to be EPS accretive by the end of 2028

 

  •  

Sanofi retains appropriate capacity to invest in early-stage BD/M&A opportunities. Sanofi confirms its progressive dividend policy and expects to maintain a strong credit rating post transaction

Paris, November 24, 2026. Sanofi today announced an agreement to acquire Insmed Incorporated (Insmed), a publicly traded, US-based global biopharmaceutical company with two commercialized medicines and a promising, innovative pipeline. The transaction is intended to broaden Sanofi’s presence in respiratory diseases, where significant unmet patient need remains.

The acquisition includes Brinsupri (brensocatib) and Arikayce (amikacin liposome inhalation suspension), along with a differentiated, phase 3 clinical program, treprostinil palmitil inhalation powder (TPIP), which has potential across multiple rare respiratory diseases. The acquisition also brings promising earlier-stage programs and research capabilities across immunology and rare diseases, reinforcing Sanofi’s own innovation and immunology capabilities globally.

Insmed is a compelling strategic fit, building on Sanofi’s longstanding experience in immunology and rare diseases. Combining Insmed’s highly differentiated portfolio and scientific expertise with Sanofi’s global reach is expected to expand patient access to important respiratory medicines while augmenting Sanofi’s commercial leadership and the rebuild of its pipeline.

This acquisition is expected to improve Sanofi’s financial profile over the mid- and long-term and expand the company’s portfolio in core focus areas.

“I am delighted to welcome Insmed to Sanofi. This acquisition is a major strategic step to significantly accelerate our mid- and long-term growth trajectory and further strengthen our mid- and early-stage pipeline,” said Belen Garijo, Chief Executive Officer of Sanofi. “Insmed builds on our already successful presence in respiratory diseases through its highly differentiated portfolio, strong science and proven commercial success. Together, we have the opportunity to build on these achievements, reach more patients globally and reinforce our leadership in diseases of significant unmet medical need. This acquisition is another example of how we are executing on our strategic roadmap with momentum to drive Sanofi’s next phase of growth.”

“The Insmed team has always set out to change what is possible for patients. In just over a year since launch, we made Brinsupri the standard of care for NCFB patients, while continuing to advance our commercial portfolio and phase 3 pipeline with a combined multibillion-dollar peak sales potential,” said Will Lewis, Chair and Chief Executive Officer of Insmed. “This transaction delivers immediate, certain cash value for Insmed shareholders and ensures the momentum we have built continues. In Sanofi we have a partner that recognizes the value of our science, commercial execution, and the exceptional culture we have created. Together, we expect to bring our therapies to more patients around the world, faster than we ever could on our own.”

Brinsupri is a small molecule, once-daily, oral, reversible inhibitor of dipeptidyl peptidase 1 (DPP1), approved in the US, EU, UK and Japan for non-cystic fibrosis bronchiectasis (NCFB), with the indicated patient populations varying by jurisdiction. Brinsupri is the first and only approved treatment for NCFB and a first-in-class DPP1 inhibitor targeting neutrophilic inflammation. Following its US launch in August 2025, Brinsupri has already reached blockbuster status, delivering $1.09 billion in revenue through September 30, 2026, with substantial additional growth expected.

Arikayce is an inhaled antibacterial treatment used in combination with multidrug therapy for certain adults with mycobacterium avium complex (MAC) lung disease and is the only approved inhaled therapy specifically indicated for treatment-refractory patients in the US, EU and Japan. Arikayce revenues were $433.8 million in 2025, representing 19% annual growth. In addition, the US Food and Drug Administration in September accepted under priority review an application for Arikayce for the treatment of MAC lung disease as part of a combination antibacterial drug regimen.

TPIP is a prostanoid being developed as a once-daily inhaled therapy for pulmonary hypertension and fibrotic lung disease. With once-daily dosing and the potential for multiple rare respiratory indications, TPIP could become a significant growth driver for Sanofi beyond 2030, subject to successful clinical development, regulatory approvals, and commercial execution.

Transaction terms and financial considerations

Under the terms of the merger agreement, Sanofi will acquire all outstanding shares of Insmed for $175 per share in cash, reflecting a total equity value of approximately $41.0 billion/ €37.6 billion.

The transaction is expected to strengthen Sanofi’s mid- to long-term growth profile. The transaction is expected to be accretive to Sanofi’s business earnings per share (EPS) by the end of 2028.

Sanofi is funding the acquisition through a combination of debt financing and cash on hand, and has secured committed financing in support of the transaction. Sanofi will maintain its conservative financial policy. The combined group’s anticipated cash generation is expected to support rapid deleveraging, while retaining flexibility to invest in early-stage BD/M&A opportunities. Sanofi remains committed to its capital allocation policy, including its progressive dividend, and expects to maintain a strong credit rating.

The transaction has been approved by the boards of directors of both companies.

The closing of the transaction is subject to approval by Insmed shareholders, receipt of U.S. and certain non-U.S. regulatory approvals and other customary conditions.

Subject to the satisfaction or waiver of these conditions, the transaction is expected to close in the first half of 2027.

The proposed acquisition is not expected to have any impact on Sanofi’s financial guidance for 2026.

About Brinsupri

Brinsupri (brensocatib) is a small molecule, once-daily, oral, reversible inhibitor of dipeptidyl peptidase 1 (DPP1), designed to inhibit the activation of neutrophil serine proteases that are key drivers of chronic airway inflammation in NCFB.

In the US and Japan, Brinsupri is approved for the treatment of NCFB in adults and pediatric patients aged 12 years and older. In the EU and UK, it is approved for patients with NCFB aged 12 years and older with two or more exacerbations in the prior 12 months.

NCFB is a serious, chronic progressive lung disease in which airways are permanently widened, causing ongoing productive cough and exacerbations. Insmed estimates that approximately 500,000 patients in the US, 600,000 in France, Germany, Italy, Spain and the UK combined, and 150,000 in Japan are diagnosed with NCFB. These estimates describe diagnosed patient populations, and some of these patients in the different jurisdictions are eligible for Brinsupri under its current labels.

Brinsupri is associated with dermatologic adverse reactions, including rash, dry skin and hyperkeratosis, and with gingival and periodontal adverse reactions. Patients should be monitored for new skin conditions and receive regular dental care. Live attenuated vaccines should be avoided during treatment.

About Arikayce

Arikayce (amikacin liposome inhalation suspension) is a novel, inhaled, once-daily liposomal formulation of amikacin, approved in the US, EU and Japan.

In the US, it is indicated as part of a combination antibacterial regimen for adults with limited or no alternative treatment options who have MAC lung disease and have not achieved negative sputum cultures after at least six consecutive months of a multidrug background regimen. Its US indication was approved under the accelerated approval pathway on the basis of sputum culture conversion. Clinical benefit has not yet been established, and continued approval may depend on verification of clinical benefit in confirmatory studies. Arikayce was approved by FDA using the limited population pathway. This means FDA has approved this medicine for a limited and specific patient population, and studies on the medicine may have only answered focused questions about its safety and effectiveness.

MAC lung disease is a rare and often chronic infection that can cause irreversible lung damage; Insmed estimates that 95,000 to 115,000 patients in the US have diagnosed MAC lung disease. These estimates describe diagnosed patient populations, not the populations eligible for Arikayce under its current labels.

Arikayce carries a US boxed warning for increased respiratory adverse reactions, including hypersensitivity pneumonitis, hemoptysis, bronchospasm and exacerbation of underlying pulmonary disease, which have led to hospitalization in some patients.

About TPIP

TPIP is a dry powder formulation of treprostinil palmitil, a treprostinil prodrug. It is being developed for once-daily administration. Its phase 3 program includes PALM-PAH in pulmonary arterial hypertension, PALM-ILD in pulmonary hypertension associated with interstitial lung disease, and PALM-PPF in progressive pulmonary fibrosis. A phase 3 study in idiopathic pulmonary fibrosis is planned to start in the first half of 2027.

TPIP is not approved for any indication in any jurisdiction. Its safety and efficacy have not been established.

About Insmed

Insmed is a people-first global biopharmaceutical company striving to deliver first- and best-in-class therapies to transform the lives of patients facing serious diseases. The Company is advancing a diverse portfolio of approved and mid- to late-stage investigational medicines — including two approved therapies to treat chronic, debilitating lung diseases — as well as cutting-edge drug discovery focused on serving patient communities where the need is greatest. Insmed’s commercial portfolio and clinical pipeline are organized around three therapeutic areas: Respiratory, Immunology & Inflammation, and Neuro & Other Rare. The Company’s research engine is advancing a wide range of technologies and modalities, including gene therapy, AI-driven protein engineering, RNA end-joining, and synthetic rescue, in the pursuit of future pipeline candidates. Headquartered in Bridgewater, New Jersey, Insmed has offices and research locations throughout the United States, Europe, and Japan.

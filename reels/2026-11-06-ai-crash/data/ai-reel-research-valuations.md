## 6. Respected voices comparing with 2000 (recent first)
- **Jim Cramer, CNBC, 5 Nov 2026** — the AI sell-off may have further to go, but it is not a dot-com repeat: the leaders are profitable and nobody big is defaulting; risk sits in borrowed-money "neoclouds" (CoreWeave, Iren, Nebius). https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/05/jim-cramer-ai-stocks-sell-off.html — Sure (read). (Commentator, not an institution.)
- **FactSet, 30 Oct 2026** — no 2000 comparison, but the key fact: index forward P/E 18.7, below its 10-yr average, because earnings grew ~30%; Mag 7 now growing slower than the other 493. (link in §1) — Sure.
- **IMF GFSR "Resilience, Risk, and Transformation", 13 Oct 2026** — markets resilient but risks elevated; weaker AI investment/profits could cause "a correction in AI-related asset prices", amplified by leverage and concentrated exposures; Guardian adds $300bn of "circular" deals between hyperscalers and weaker AI firms. No explicit dot-com comparison found. https://www.imf.org/en/publications/gfsr/issues/2026/10/13/global-financial-stability-report-october-2026 · https://www.resultsense.com/news/2026-10-13-imf-ai-share-correction-global-stability/ — Sure (IMF page read; figures via press).
- **BofA Global Fund Manager Survey, 13 Oct 2026** — net 37% overweight tech (most since Aug 2020); 45% expect an AI bubble to form in 2027 vs 42% no; semiconductors the most crowded trade (45%). https://cryptobriefing.com/bofa-survey-fund-managers-overweight-tech/ — Snippet only, single secondary source: NOT confirmed.
- **Amundi (Roncalli), 9 Jun 2026** — "less like the dot-com bubble", more a market dependent on a few giants: in 1999 P/Es often >50x on hopes; today leaders earn real profits and have less debt (debt/capital <25%). (link in §3) — Sure.
- **Fortune "1997 or 1999?", 18 May 2026** — Evercore's Emanuel: "feels like 1999" (but "high, but not Y2K extremes" per Yahoo 12 May); Goldman (Mei): breadth "far less narrow" than 1999–2000; Dan Niles: looks like 1997; Morgan Stanley (Wilson): an earnings story, not multiple expansion. (link in §5) — Sure.
- **Yahoo Finance, 12 May 2026** — Boockvar: index record while 5% of members hit 52-week lows, seen only in Jul 1929, Jan 1973 and Dec 1999. (link in §4) — Sure.
- Older but often cited: **Apollo (Sløk), Jul 2025** — top-10 more overvalued than in the 1990s (https://finance.yahoo.com/news/apollo-chief-economist-warns-ai-154650813.html, read); **Bank of England FPC, Oct 2025** — valuations "stretched", especially AI firms (snippet; no Oct 2026 FPC statement found); **Goldman, Oct 2025** — top-5 P/E ~28x vs ~50x at the 2000 peak (snippet only).

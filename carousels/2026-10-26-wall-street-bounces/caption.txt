Wall Street closed higher on Monday, after two losing weeks in a row for the S&P 500 and the Nasdaq.

The S&P 500 rose 0.7%, to 7,676.77. The Nasdaq 100 gained 1.1%, to 29,273.40, and the Dow Jones 0.3%, to 52,361.58.

Technology led the US sectors, up 1.7%, followed by industrials (+1.4%). Communication services fell the most, 1.6%. The Philadelphia Semiconductor index rose 2.8%. Chip and AI-related stocks rose after Anthropic publicly filed for an IPO, Yahoo Finance and Capital Brief reported.

US crude (WTI) fell 2.0%, to $101.25 a barrel, and the VIX volatility index 5.4%, to 18.39.

The week ahead: the Federal Reserve's two-day meeting starts Tuesday, with its rate decision on Wednesday. Alphabet and Microsoft report their results on Wednesday, after the close.

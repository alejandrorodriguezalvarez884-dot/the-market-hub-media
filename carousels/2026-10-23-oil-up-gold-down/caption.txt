Oil up, gold down: two of the world's most-watched prices are going opposite ways this Friday morning.

Brent crude, the global oil benchmark, is at $114.16 a barrel, up 0.9% today and its highest since early May. It ended 2025 at $60.85, so it is up about 88% this year; its highest close of 2026 was $118.35, on March 31. US crude (WTI) is at $100.77, up 1.3% today.

The latest jump came after a drone attack in Saudi Arabia. On Wednesday, Saudi air defences intercepted a drone near a processing plant at the Shaybah oil field; its debris hit a vehicle outside and two people were killed, according to The National. The Saudi Energy Ministry said: "Operations were unaffected."

Gold is down 0.8% to $4,030 an ounce, its lowest since July. It peaked at $5,318 on January 29 and is now about 24% below that level. Its lowest close this year was $3,992, on July 16. Silver is down 1.6% to $53.91.

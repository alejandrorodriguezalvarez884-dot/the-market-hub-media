Before the bell: five big companies reported results before the US open on Thursday, October 22, and Nebius signed a loan facility with Citi.

Spotify: 788 million monthly users in the third quarter, up 11%, and 306 million subscribers, up 9%. Revenue €5.0 billion, up 16%; operating income €672 million, up 15%. Gross margin of 33.0%, its highest for a third quarter. Premarket: +5.9%.

Lockheed Martin: sales of $20.7 billion, up 11%; net earnings $1.8 billion, up 14%. A record $292 billion backlog, $52.5 billion of it from a multi-year PAC-3 MSE interceptor award. It raised its 2026 outlook for sales, EPS and free cash flow. Premarket: +4.4%.

Procter & Gamble: sales of $23.3 billion in July to September, its fiscal first quarter, up 4%, with organic sales also up 4%. Diluted EPS $1.97, up 1%. It kept its full-year guidance. Premarket: +1.3%.

Honeywell: its first quarter as a standalone automation company. Sales $5.0 billion, up 1%, or 7% organic; operating margin 14.8%, from 8.4%. Adjusted EPS $2.27, up 34%; reported EPS $2.40, down 20%, against a one-off gain a year earlier. It raised its 2026 adjusted EPS outlook to $8.40-$8.45. Premarket: +0.8% at 7:12am ET.

Blackstone: $63.3 billion of inflows and $1.37 trillion of assets under management. Distributable earnings of $1.32 a share, from $1.52 a year earlier. Dividend of $1.12 a share. Premarket: -3.8%.

Nebius: a $3.5 billion senior secured facility from Citi, for 364 days, at Term SOFR plus 2.75%, a margin that can rise to 3.50%. It is for data centers, GPUs and general corporate purposes. Premarket: -2.9%.

Premarket moves at 8:30am ET unless noted, against the October 21 close.

Sources: the companies' releases filed with the SEC on October 22, 2026 (Forms 8-K and 6-K); prices, Yahoo Finance.

Europe's chipmakers opened lower on Friday. At 9:23 AM CET the Euro Stoxx 50 was down 0.8%, at 6,155.25, against Thursday's close of 6,207.07. Frankfurt's DAX was down 0.3%, while London's FTSE 100 was up 0.3%.

Five large chip stocks were all lower: ASML down 5.1%, ASM International 4.8%, BE Semiconductor 4.5%, Infineon 4.0% and STMicroelectronics 3.8%. In London, BP rose 1.4% and Shell 0.6%, with Brent crude futures up 1.6% at $112.53 a barrel.

It came after two US results released on Thursday after the close. Intel's revenue rose 21% to $16.5 billion in the third quarter, and it forecast $15.0 to $16.0 billion for the fourth; its shares were 26.4% lower in premarket trading early on Friday. Amazon's net sales rose 12% to $201.9 billion; its shares were 7.3% lower in after-hours trading. Nasdaq 100 futures were down 2.2% at 9:28 AM CET.

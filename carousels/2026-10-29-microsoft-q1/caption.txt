Microsoft shares were 3.8% higher in premarket trading on Thursday morning, after its results for the quarter to September 30.

Revenue rose 19% from a year earlier, to $92.3 billion. Operating income rose 21% to $45.9 billion, and diluted earnings per share rose 32% to $4.92.

Microsoft Cloud revenue rose 30% to $63.9 billion, and Azure revenue rose 48%. The Agents and Infra segment brought in $76.8 billion (+25%); Devices and Consumer, $15.5 billion (-3%).

Additions to property and equipment were $39.4 billion in the quarter, against $19.4 billion a year earlier. Commercial remaining performance obligation rose 73% to $677 billion.

Oil is easing on Monday morning, after a 6% jump last week.

Brent crude was at $115.21 a barrel at 08:30 UTC, 1.3% below Friday's close of $116.72. That close was its highest since April 29, after a 6.2% rise over the week. US crude (WTI) was 1.9% lower, at $101.33.

Qatari mediators travelled to Tehran on Sunday, in efforts to ease tensions between the US and Iran, according to Grafa. Houthi attacks on Saudi Arabian oil facilities and infrastructure continued over the weekend, it reported.

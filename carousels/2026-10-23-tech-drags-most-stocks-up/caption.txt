The S&P 500 had its worst day since July on Thursday, yet most of its stocks rose.

The index closed 1.2% lower, at 7,663.43. The tech-heavy Nasdaq 100 lost 2.9%, while the Dow Jones ended flat, up less than 0.1%. Tech stocks sold off on worries about AI spending and the cost of capital, according to Yahoo Finance. Nvidia, the largest company on Wall Street by total value, fell 4.4%.

Away from tech, the day looked different. Six of the eleven US sectors rose: Consumer Defensive gained 3.3%, Utilities 2.1% and Healthcare 1.3%. Technology was the weakest, down 3.3%, followed by Communication Services, down 1.4%.

Money also moved into US government bonds. The 10-year Treasury yield fell to 4.96% from 5.13% on Wednesday, and from more than 5.30% earlier this month, according to AP. The 2-year yield fell to 4.65% from 4.81%. When buyers pay more for a bond, its yield falls. Brent crude oil rose 4% on Thursday.

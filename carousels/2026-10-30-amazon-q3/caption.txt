Amazon shares were 7.3% lower in after-hours trading on Thursday, after its results for the quarter to September 30. The last after-hours price was $225.99, against Thursday's close of $243.79.

Net sales rose 12% from a year earlier, to $201.9 billion. North America sales rose 6% to $112.9 billion, International 5% to $43.1 billion, and AWS 39% to $46.0 billion.

Operating income was $28.1 billion, against $17.4 billion a year earlier. Net income was $20.7 billion, or $1.89 per diluted share, against $21.2 billion, or $1.95; the company says the year-earlier quarter included $9.5 billion of pre-tax gains from its investment in Anthropic.

Purchases of property and equipment were $77.3 billion in the quarter, against $35.1 billion a year earlier. Amazon says it continues to expect 2026 cash capital expenditures of about $220 billion. For the fourth quarter it expects net sales of $240.0 to $247.0 billion.

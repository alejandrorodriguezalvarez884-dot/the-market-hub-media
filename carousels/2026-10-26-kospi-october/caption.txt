Seoul's Kospi has lost a fifth of its value in October.

South Korea's main index closed 1.5% lower on Monday, at 5,469.47, its lowest close since early April. On September 30 it closed at 6,838.04, so it is down 20.0% this month. It is 40% below its peak of 9,114.55, reached on June 22.

The country's chipmakers fell further. SK hynix is down 28.2% in October and Samsung Electronics 20.7%, by their daily closes. On Monday, foreign investors were net sellers of 362.1 billion won of Korean shares by late morning, according to IBTimes.

Both report their third-quarter results this week: SK hynix on Wednesday, October 28, the same day the US Federal Reserve announces its rate decision, and Samsung Electronics on Thursday.

In Tokyo, the Nikkei 225 closed 0.8% lower, at 62,189.65. It was its third fall in a row and its lowest close since July 30, with chip-related shares falling again, according to Reuters.

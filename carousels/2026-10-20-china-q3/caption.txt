China grew 4.5% in the third quarter.

Official data released on October 19: GDP rose 4.5% from a year earlier in Q3, up from 4.3% in Q2 and 5.0% in Q1, and 4.6% for the first nine months. It grew 1.2% on the previous quarter.

In September, industrial output rose 5.5% and retail sales 1.1% from a year earlier; the urban surveyed unemployment rate was 5.2%. From January to September, exports rose 16.4% in yuan, while fixed-asset investment fell 7.2% and property investment 20.6%.

A day later, on October 20, China's loan prime rates were left unchanged: 3.0% for one year and 3.5% for over five years, the benchmark many mortgages are priced on.

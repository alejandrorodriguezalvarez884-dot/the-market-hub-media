The Fed held its interest rate on Wednesday.

The Federal Reserve kept the target range for the federal funds rate at 3.75% to 4.00%. That is where it set it on September 16, when it raised the range by a quarter of a percentage point.

The vote was 10 to 2. Lisa D. Cook and Beth M. Hammack voted against: they preferred to raise the range by a quarter point at this meeting.

The statement says economic activity "is expanding at a strong pace", that "job gains have kept pace with the workforce, and the unemployment rate has changed little", and that "inflation remains elevated". The Fed says more information will help it decide the stance that returns inflation to its 2% goal in a timely way.

The committee meets next on December 8 and 9.

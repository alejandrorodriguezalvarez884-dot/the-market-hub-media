Options. Finally readable.

Options Lab, now in My Hub, reads the option chain of any US-listed stock or fund:

- Every call and put: implied volatility, the greeks, breakeven and the odds the prices imply.
- Each expiration at a glance: expected move, one-sigma range, 25-delta skew, put/call open interest and volume, max pain.
- The volatility smile, open interest by strike and the term structure across every expiration.
- Strategies of up to 8 legs (calls, puts and shares), from a template or leg by leg: payoff at expiration and before it, breakevens, max profit and loss, greeks and the probability of profit. Save them and follow them from your account.

Every figure is computed in code with Black-Scholes-Merton from Yahoo Finance prices. The probabilities are the ones option prices imply, not a forecast. The screenshots show NVIDIA on October 14, 2026; the iron condor is only an example of what the tool draws.

Sign in at themarkethub.app, then My Hub > Options.

Market Hub describes. It never tells you what to buy or sell, and nothing here is investment advice.

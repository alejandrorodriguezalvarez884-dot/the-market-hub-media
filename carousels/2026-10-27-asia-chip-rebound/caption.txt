Asia's stock markets bounced on Tuesday, led by chipmakers.

Seoul's Kospi closed 3.1% higher, at 5,638.34, a day after its lowest close since early April. It had been up more than 4% during the session, AP reported. Even after Tuesday, it is down 17.5% in October: it closed September at 6,838.04.

Tokyo's Nikkei 225 rose 1.7%, to 63,268.37.

Chip and tech stocks did most of the lifting. In Tokyo, chip-testing equipment maker Advantest jumped 9.6% and SoftBank Group climbed 5.0%, according to AP. In Seoul, SK hynix rose 5.2% and Samsung Electronics 4.2%, by their daily closes.

Not every market rose: Hong Kong's Hang Seng slipped 0.6% and the Shanghai Composite fell 0.3%, AP reported.

SK hynix reports its third-quarter results on Wednesday, October 28, and Samsung Electronics on Thursday, October 29.

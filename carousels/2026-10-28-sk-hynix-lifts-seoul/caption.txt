Seoul's stock market jumped on Wednesday, on the day SK hynix reported a record quarter.

The Kospi closed 6.6% higher, at 6,012.44, back above 6,000. Even so, it is down 12.1% in October: it closed September at 6,838.04.

SK hynix's preliminary third-quarter results came out before trading, AFP reported. Revenue was 104.6 trillion won, up 328% from 24.4 trillion a year earlier. Operating profit was 83.3 trillion won, up 632% from 11.4 trillion. That is an operating margin of 80%, from 47% in the third quarter of 2025. The company also set out about 100 trillion won of shareholder returns for 2026, in dividends and share buybacks.

Its shares closed 10.9% higher. Samsung Electronics rose 9.0%; it reports its own third quarter on Thursday, October 29.

Elsewhere in Asia, Tokyo's Nikkei 225 rose 1.1%, to 63,954.19, and the Shanghai Composite 0.9%.

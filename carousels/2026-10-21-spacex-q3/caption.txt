SpaceX's third quarter, in three numbers.

Revenue was $14.6 billion in the three months to September 30, up 161% from $5.6 billion a year earlier.

More than half came from AI: the Super Intelligence segment, mostly computing capacity rented out, brought in $7.6 billion (+830%). Connectivity, which is Starlink, $5.2 billion (+69%), with 13.6 million consumer subscribers against 7.2 million a year before. Space, the launch business, $1.8 billion (+6%).

Net income was $4.4 billion, after a $1.1 billion loss in the same quarter of 2025. Adjusted EBITDA rose 306% to $8.8 billion. Capital spending was $19.6 billion, $16.4 billion of it on AI.

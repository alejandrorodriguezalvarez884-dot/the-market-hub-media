Four of Europe's big banks reported their third quarter on Wednesday morning, and their shares went two ways.

In the first hours of trading, Standard Chartered was 4.4% higher in London and Deutsche Bank 3.4% higher in Frankfurt. UBS was 1.7% lower in Zurich and Santander 3.4% lower in Madrid. The Stoxx Europe 600 was down 0.2%. (Prices at 08:23 UTC, against Tuesday's close.)

Standard Chartered: pre-tax profit rose 27%, to $2.25 billion from $1.77 billion, and the bank now expects its 2026 income to grow by more than 7%.

Deutsche Bank: post-tax profit of €1.9 billion, a record for a third quarter. Pre-tax profit rose 8%, to €2.6 billion, and revenue 4%, to €8.3 billion.

UBS: net profit rose 8%, to $2.69 billion from $2.48 billion. It bought back $1.8 billion of its shares in the quarter, under a $3 billion plan.

Santander: underlying profit rose 19%, to €3.8 billion. Its attributable profit for the first nine months was €12.4 billion, up 20%.

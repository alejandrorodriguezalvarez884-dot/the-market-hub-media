Volvo Cars was down 27% in Stockholm at 1pm local time, at its lowest since it listed in 2021.

Its Q3: revenue SEK 66.9B, from SEK 86.4B a year earlier, and an adjusted operating loss of SEK 3.7B, from a SEK 5.8B profit.

It will cut about 2,500 jobs, 2,000 of them in Sweden.

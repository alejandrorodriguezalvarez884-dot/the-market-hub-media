Euro area GDP grew 0.4% in the third quarter from the second, Eurostat's first estimate says. It grew 0.6% in the second.

Spain grew 0.6% and Germany 0.2%. Italy was flat, and France shrank 0.1%.

From a year earlier, the euro area grew 1.3%.

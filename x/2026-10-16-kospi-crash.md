South Korea's KOSPI fell 7.6% on Thursday to 5,554, its seventh drop in a row. A circuit breaker halted trading for 20 minutes.

SK Hynix fell 11.1%, Samsung 9.5%.

That left it 39% below its June 22 peak of 9,115. A year ago it stood near 3,750.

Today it rose 2.0%.

Tokyo's Nikkei 225 fell as much as 4.8% on Wednesday, to 60,030, its lowest since May, as early US midterm results pointed to Democratic gains in Congress.

It won back most of that and closed 1.4% lower, at 62,188.63. Chip and AI-related shares took the biggest hit.

US GDP grew at a 3.4% annual rate in the third quarter, the first official estimate says. It grew 2.2% in the second.

Investment, consumer spending and government spending rose; exports fell.

PCE prices rose at a 2.1% rate, from 5.0%. Without food and energy: 2.5%, from 3.3%.

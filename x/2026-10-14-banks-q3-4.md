Wells Fargo: net income $5.95bn, +6%; EPS $1.87, +13%. Revenue $21.87bn, +2%.

Loans and deposits both grew 11%. M&A revenue rose 30% and equity underwriting fees 33%. Markets revenue was roughly flat, though equities grew 21%. It bought back $2.5bn of stock.

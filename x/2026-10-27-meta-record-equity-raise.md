Meta has lifted its largest-ever equity raise to $97.5 billion.

Priced: $25 billion of common shares at $625 each, below Monday's $630.56 close, and $12.5 billion of preferred paying 6.50% that convert into common.

Still to come: a sale of up to $60 billion, timing not set.
